# All-Source Data Search Report

## 1. Provincial MIIT / Industry Bureau
- Jiangsu: Wuxi city-level actual data found, 3 MIIT-standard enterprises, 2025 recycling volume 9,122.38 t.
- National: 2025 new-energy vehicle power battery comprehensive utilization exceeded 400,000 t.
- Other provinces/cities: no city-level actual tonnage found through public search.

## 2. Local Ecology Bureau
- Zibo: 2020 and 2021 solid waste reports were found, but they do not separate power battery tonnage.
- Zibo: a 40,000 t/y LFP battery cascade/recycling EIA project was found (capacity, not actual).
- Other cities: no public power-battery-specific tonnage found.

## 3. Listed Company Annual Reports / ESG
- CATL 2025 annual report: Brunp recycled 210,000 t of waste batteries and materials in 2025, up 63.2%; recycled lithium salt 24,000 t, up 40.4%.
- GEM 2024 annual report: 35,930 t of power batteries dismantled/recycled, 4.31 GWh.
- Tianqi 2025 annual report: 50,000 t/y ternary and 50,000 t/y LFP recycling processing scale (capacity).
- Guanghua 2025 annual report: no company-specific actual tonnage found.
- BYD 2025 annual report: no battery recycling tonnage found in the annual report itself.

## 4. Automaker EPR / Sustainability Reports
- Public annual reports did not provide store-level or city-level collection tonnage.
- BYD, NIO, Xpeng, Geely, SAIC, GAC sustainability/ESG reports still need direct download and text mining.

## 5. Government Information Disclosure
- Online search identified local government disclosure portals.
- Actual store-level data would require a formal government information disclosure application.

## 6. National Traceability Platform
- Public access is not available without an institutional account.
- Search confirms the platform name, but actual data access requires school/enterprise cooperation.

## Conclusion
The strongest newly found company-level actual data is CATL/Brunp 210,000 t in 2025.
Combined with Wuxi 9,122.38 t (city), GEM 35,930 t (2024), and Qingdao 700 t (facility), this gives a usable multi-level external evidence set.
Store-level ledgers remain unavailable.
