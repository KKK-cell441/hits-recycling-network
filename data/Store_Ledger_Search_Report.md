## Findings
1. No actual store-level 4S power battery recycling ledger was found in public sources.
2. Search results are dominated by ledger templates for lead-acid batteries, general battery collection forms, and regulatory notices requiring record-keeping.
3. Public records confirm that service points are required to record battery source, code, type, quantity, destination, and vehicle VIN, and to retain records for three years.
4. MIIT data cited by AskCI (2025-08-11) show 10,908 national power battery recycling service points, with Guangdong 1,102, Jiangsu >850, Zhejiang 776, Shandong 758, and Henan/Sichuan >550.
5. Wuxi city-level actual recycling volume was found separately: 3 MIIT-standard enterprises reported 9,122.38 tonnes in 2025.

## Conclusion
Store-level ledgers are not publicly available. The closest usable public evidence is:
- city-level actual volume for Wuxi (9,122.38 t);
- national and provincial service point counts;
- company-level annual report figures such as GEM 35,930 t.

## Recommendation
Use Wuxi as a second city-level external validation case. Use service point counts as a network coverage cross-check. Keep store-level ledger validation as future work.
