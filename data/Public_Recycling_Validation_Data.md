## Notes
1. The GEM figure is the most useful actual company-level validation point: 35,930 tonnes of power batteries were dismantled/recycled in 2024.
2. CATL, Tianqi, and Guanghua figures are industry-level totals cited inside company reports, not company-specific volumes.
3. CNGR reports capacity, not actual throughput.
4. Huayou's 3,227 tonnes refers to non-power-battery waste and is not directly usable.
5. No store-level 4S recycling ledgers were found in these public annual reports.

## Recommendation
Use GEM 35,930 t (2024) as an actual company-level validation reference and label the remaining data as industry-level or capacity-level support.
